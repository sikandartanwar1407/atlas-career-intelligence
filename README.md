# ATLAS — Career Intelligence Platform

> **From career goals to demonstrated capability.**

ATLAS is a Career Intelligence Platform designed to help students and early-career professionals understand their current capabilities, identify skill gaps, build targeted learning roadmaps, produce evidence of their skills, and become discoverable to employers through consent-based matching.

Instead of treating a resume as the complete representation of a candidate, ATLAS creates a continuous intelligence loop:

**Profile → Career Goal → Assessment → Diagnosis → Skill Gaps → Roadmap → Build → Evidence → Reassessment**

---

## 🚀 Why ATLAS?

Many learners face the same problem:

- They don't know which skills their target career actually requires.
- They learn through scattered courses without knowing what to prioritize.
- Traditional assessments often test theoretical knowledge but not applied capability.
- Resumes rarely provide enough evidence of what a candidate can actually do.
- Recruiters have difficulty distinguishing stated skills from demonstrated capability.

ATLAS addresses this by connecting assessment, learning, evidence, and employer discovery into one system.
